Borders separate, shadows are only for things that float (menus, the save bar, dialogs). The focus ring is the same 2 px accent outline on every control.

Buttons

One solid primary per page. The tinted top-bar button is the global action and is never styled like a page primary. Destructive buttons are outlined and sit apart; the solid red appears only inside a confirmation dialog.

One unified diff everywhere: Publish, History, rollback and compare. The + and − signs carry the meaning and are not selectable, so copied text is clean. A changed secret gets its own line and never shows a value.
