Delete Moodle?

Nodes keep serving it until you publish. Until then you can discard the deletion.

Discard all 7 changes?

3 modified, 2 new, 1 withdrawn and 1 deleted object go back to what is live. This cannot be undone.

Leave without saving?

Your edits to 1 option will be lost.

Only for delete, discard, rollback and leaving unsaved edits. The button repeats the verb. Without the script it is a page of its own.
